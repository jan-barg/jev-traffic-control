# Draft only: supporting data for a Midtown South traffic simulation

Status: prepared locally on 21 September 2026; not sent or submitted.

Suggested destination: NYC DOT/DCP records or study-data staff, using the official [DOT records-request process](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/about/foil.shtml) if appropriate. This is a technical draft, not a claim about legal entitlement or a guarantee that records exist.

Subject: Existing-condition traffic inputs for Midtown South FEIS, CEQR 24DCP094M

We are preparing a research simulation comparing signal-control methods. We seek existing electronic records supporting the June 2024 existing-condition traffic network in Chapter 13 of the Midtown South Mixed-Use Plan FEIS, particularly the following twelve intersections:

- Sixth Avenue at West 25th, 26th, 27th, 28th, 29th and 30th Streets (FEIS intersection IDs 14, 13, 12, 11, 10 and 9).
- Seventh Avenue at West 25th, 26th, 27th, 28th, 29th and 30th Streets (FEIS IDs 28, 27, 26, 25, 24 and 23).

Please provide, where maintained:

1. The actual traffic-count dates, original 15-minute turning movements, vehicle classifications, ATR counts, pedestrian/cycle counts and count-location identifiers used for the existing-condition network. Please identify figures reused from other studies, balancing/rounding adjustments, exclusions and missing periods.
2. Applicable signal timing sheets and controller schedules on those dates: phase diagrams, cycle lengths, splits, offsets and reference convention, yellow/all-red timing, pedestrian walk/clearance/LPI settings, minimum/maximum greens and detector/actuation settings. Include records of any adaptive operation or overrides affecting those dates, if retained.
3. Supporting existing-condition Synchro files, relevant roadway/crosswalk inventories, lane-use/turn restrictions and effective dates. We seek the observed-period inputs, rather than only future No-Action, With-Action or mitigation settings.
4. Available measured travel-time, queue or delay studies for those dates and nearby dates, including methodology, sampling intervals, measurement endpoints and sample sizes. Please distinguish measurements from HCM/Synchro calculated delay.
5. Available historical aggregate observations for local-street travel-time links 047023 (Sixth Avenue northbound, 23rd–34th) and 060106 (Seventh Avenue southbound, 34th–23rd), their timestamp timezone, sample inclusion rules, missing-data codes and segment geometry. Aggregate observations are sufficient; individual E-ZPass identifiers are not requested.

The analysis periods of interest are weekday 08:00–09:00, 12:00–13:00 and 17:00–18:00, and Saturday 13:00–14:00. If available, records for the surrounding hour would help model entry queues and warm-up. Nearby intersections between 23rd and 34th Streets are relevant to boundary effects and travel-time matching.

If the records are maintained by another unit or under a different identifier, please identify the relevant custodian or public download location. Native electronic tables and model files are preferred where they already exist.

Before sending, supply the requester's contact information and choose the intended request route. No identity or contact details have been invented in this draft.
